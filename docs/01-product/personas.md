# Personas

These are working sketches to design against, not research findings. The employer persona comes from the real conversation that started the project; the candidates are assumptions.

## Employer: the hotel HR manager (anchor case)
- **Situation:** runs hiring for a hotel. Needs housekeeping, kitchen, reception and seasonal staff, often at short notice.
- **Pain:** posts reach people who live too far away. Good applicants drop out when they work out the commute.
- **Wants:** people who live close, who answer quickly, and who can start soon.
- **Uses the app to:** post a role in a minute, see who applied with their distance, accept the promising ones and message them.
- **Will leave if:** applicants are irrelevant, or nobody applies within a day.

## Employer: the small business owner
- **Situation:** owns a café, shop or workshop. No HR function; hiring happens between serving customers.
- **Pain:** hires through word of mouth and a sign in the window.
- **Wants:** the digital version of the sign in the window, seen by the neighbourhood.
- **Uses the app to:** post once, check applicants in the evening.
- **Will leave if:** posting takes more than a couple of minutes or asks for documents up front.

## Candidate: the nearby job seeker
- **Situation:** looking for work, any reasonable job, and has no car or a long commute is costly.
- **Pain:** job boards show jobs across the country; filtering by place is clumsy.
- **Wants:** to know what is hiring within reach, and to hear about new openings first.
- **Uses the app to:** swipe through nearby jobs in a spare minute, respond to pushes.
- **Will leave if:** the stack is empty, or right swipes never get an answer.

## Candidate: the student or part-timer
- **Situation:** wants hours that fit around study, close to campus or home.
- **Wants:** contract type and hours visible on the card.
- **Will leave if:** every card is a full-time role.

## Candidate: the professional who wants a shorter commute
- **Situation:** employed, open to the same job closer to home.
- **Wants:** to browse quietly; low notification volume.
- **Design consequence:** notifications must be easy to tune down without turning the app off.

## What the personas imply
- Distance and pay go at the top of every job card.
- Contract type is a first-class field, not buried in the description.
- Employers see a distance bucket per applicant, because distance is what they are buying.
- Response time matters to both sides, so it is measured from day one even if it is not shown yet.
